On the menu

Two signature blends and everything we brew starts here


Golden Roast

Bright and delicate our lighter house blend carries honey and stone-fruit notes, roasted just past first crack. Best as a pour-over, no sugar needed.

Velvet Brew

Dark, silky, and low in acidity. Cocoa and toasted hazelnut lead every note the blend we pour our heart into, one latte at a time.
